Privacy Policy

Bounce Rally Privacy Policy

Bounce Rally is a single-player casual game with no accounts, advertising, or analytics. Your scores and preferences are stored only on your device.

1. Overview

We follow the principle of data minimization. The app's core features do not require registration or sign-in, and you do not need to provide us with your name, phone number, email address, or other identity information.

May leave your device when enabled

  • Device registration tokens and basic diagnostic information needed for push notifications
  • Your IP address and standard network information when requesting announcements

Stored only on your device

  • High scores, best combos, and games played
  • Unlocked achievements
  • Sound, music, haptics, and reduced-motion preferences

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following game progress and preferences locally on your device:

  • High scores, best combos, and total games played;
  • Unlocked achievements;
  • Sound, music, haptic feedback, and reduced-motion preferences.

This information is not uploaded to our servers, and we cannot access it remotely. You can select Settings → Reset Progress in the app to clear scores, games played, and achievements. Deleting the app removes all data stored by it on your device.

If you enable iCloud or Finder device backups, an Apple backup may include this local data. Those backups are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy and are outside our control.

3. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses messaging services provided by Apple and Google to deliver them. To deliver messages, these services may process device registration identifiers and basic diagnostic information. We do not combine this information with your identity or use it for tracking or advertising.

Refusing or disabling notifications does not affect the app's core features. You can manage notification permissions at any time in your iOS settings.

4. In-app web pages

The app may display service notices or announcements. When requesting this content, the server may receive your IP address, request time, and standard network information, but it will not receive your game progress or identity information.

The app does not persist cookies or website data created by announcement pages. If a page is operated by a third party, that party's privacy policy applies to its data practices.

5. Information we do not collect

The app:

  • No accounts or sign-in: we do not ask you to submit your name, phone number, email address, or password;
  • No advertising: we do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising;
  • No behavioral analytics: we do not measure your taps, sessions, or gameplay;
  • No cross-app tracking;
  • No payment information: there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions;
  • No sensitive device permissions: we do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, health data, or calendar;
  • No social features: there are no leaderboards, chat, or user-generated content.

6. Third-party services and international processing

Push notifications are supported by Apple and Google. These service providers may process information needed to deliver messages outside your country or region. We use these services only to provide push notifications and do not sell personal information to third parties.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy for more information about their data practices.

7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and contains no advertising, purchases, chat, or user-generated content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or other minors.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a minor's personal information has been provided to us, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy. After verification, we will handle or delete the information as required by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Clear local data: use the in-app "Reset Progress" option or delete the app;
  • Manage notifications: allow or disable notifications in your iOS settings;
  • Manage web data: announcement pages use a temporary session, and the app does not persist cookies or website data after you close them;
  • Make a privacy request: contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information, or to withdraw consent, as permitted by law.

Because we do not provide user accounts and local game data is not uploaded, in most cases we do not hold information that can identify you or be used to retrieve your data. We may ask for information necessary to verify your request and respond as required by law.

9. Retention and security

Local device data is retained until you reset your progress or delete the app. Push tokens are used only while valid for message delivery and may become invalid after an app reinstall, device restore, or service-provider rotation. Server logs, if any, are retained only as long as needed for operations, security, and legal obligations.

The app uses secure connections for network communications. Although no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, we take reasonable measures appropriate to the nature of the information to protect it from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, data practices, or applicable laws change. Updated content will be posted on this page. If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide additional notice where appropriate.
